# Privacy policy for Imbaxis

**Effective date:** 25 September 2026

Imbaxis ("we") provides the Imbaxis app. This policy explains what we collect and why.

## Information we collect

When you sign in with Google and create a profile, we collect:

- Your email address
- Your gamer tag
- Your age
- A Friend ID
- An account ID from Epic Online Services
- Your coin and gem balance

While you play, the app also handles match data needed to run an online game: seat names, Friend IDs, moves, chat, and reactions. That data is sent between players in the match.

The app may show a notification while a match is running so the game can stay connected if you switch apps. That notification stops when the match ends.

## How we use it

We use this information to sign you in, keep your profile and balances, run online matches, let players add friends by Friend ID, and enforce the 13+ age requirement.

We do not sell your information.

## Who we share it with

- **Google**, to sign you in.
- **Epic Games (Epic Online Services)**, to store your account, profile, and coin and gem ledger, and to connect online matches.
- **Other players in your match**, who can see your gamer tag, Friend ID, chat, and reactions.

Friend search uses Friend ID only. Other players cannot search for you by email.

## Age

You must be 13 or older to create an account. Imbaxis is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## How long we keep it

Profile and balance data stay in your account until you ask us to delete them, or until we no longer need them to run the game. Match data exists to run the current match.

## Deletion

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. This removes your profile, Friend ID, coin and gem balance, and saved game data from the app and from Epic Online Services, then signs you out. Uninstalling the app does not delete the online account.

You can also email **[YOUR EMAIL HERE]** from the address on the account and include your Friend ID or gamer tag. We will delete the same account data.

## Security

Account and ledger data are sent over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.
